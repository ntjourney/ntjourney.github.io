2. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the NextRound website, web app and mobile app. NextRound is a party and gaming application with multiplayer lobbies, optional user accounts and optional premium features.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use advertising SDKs, Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Sentry, Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel or comparable analytics and advertising services.

3. Accessing the Website

Our website is provided through GitHub Pages. When you access it, technically necessary access data is processed, in particular your IP address, date and time, requested file or page, amount of data transferred, browser type, operating system and, where applicable, the previously visited page.

Processing serves to provide, secure and maintain the stability of the website. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is the secure and reliable operation of the website.

Provider: GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. Further information: GitHub Privacy Statement.

4. User Account and Gameplay

Use Without an Account

Free use is generally possible without registration. In this case, we process your chosen player name as well as technically necessary session and lobby data to the extent required for the relevant game round.

User Account

An account is required for certain features, in particular premium features and use across multiple devices. For this purpose, we process in particular your email address, authentication data, profile settings as well as premium and subscription status.

The purpose is to provide and manage the account and to assign features and premium access. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Multiplayer, Lobbies and Statistics

We process player names, lobby codes, player and session information, votes, scores, game-related assignments, round results, XP, levels and other associated game statistics. Processing serves to create, run and synchronize game rounds as well as to display results, game statistics and game-related progress.

The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR where processing is required for the use of NextRound, the conduct of game rounds or the provision of account and gameplay features used by you.

Your player name is visible to other participants in the same lobby. Therefore, do not use a name containing sensitive personal information.

Friends Feature

NextRound offers an optional friends feature. You can add other users as friends using their individual friend code or by scanning a QR code provided by them.

In order to provide the friends feature, we process your player name, your individual friend code, the friendship connection between user accounts and technical information required to manage the friendship. If you enter another user's friend code or scan their QR code, that code is processed in order to create or assign the friendship.

Friends may see each other's player name, current online or offline status, current level and XP. Conversely, you can see this information for your friends.

Processing serves to provide and manage the friends feature used by you and related social and gameplay features within NextRound. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.

You can end or remove a friendship at any time in the profile section. After a friendship ends, your profile will no longer be visible to that person through the friends feature, online status or Friends Leaderboard.

Community Leaderboard

NextRound may provide a Community Leaderboard. Your player name, current level and XP may be displayed there to other NextRound users.

Your profile is displayed in the Community Leaderboard only if you enable this feature in your profile settings. Participation is voluntary and is not required to use NextRound. The legal basis is your consent pursuant to Article 6(1)(a) GDPR.

You can disable display in the Community Leaderboard at any time with effect for the future in your profile settings. After deactivation, your profile will no longer be displayed in the Community Leaderboard. Technically cached displays may remain visible for a short period where technically necessary.

Friends Leaderboard

If you use the friends feature, your friends may see your player name, current level and XP in a Friends Leaderboard. The Friends Leaderboard displays only users with whom a friendship exists.

Display in the Friends Leaderboard serves to provide the friends feature used by you and its related gameplay comparison function. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Separate deactivation of the Friends Leaderboard is not provided. However, you can end or remove a friendship at any time in the profile section. After the friendship ends, your profile will no longer be visible to that person in the Friends Leaderboard.

Feedback and Technical Data

If you send feedback, we process the text you enter and, if you are logged in, the association with your account. This serves to fix errors, evaluate suggestions and improve NextRound. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

For operation, stability, error analysis and prevention of misuse, we may process IP addresses, device information, access times and server logs. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

6. Sign in with Google

You may voluntarily sign in with Google Sign-In where this login method is available. You will be redirected to Google. Google provides us with the information required for login, in particular your email address and an account identifier. We do not receive your Google account password.

The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. The purpose is to create and sign in to a user account. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Google also processes data under its own responsibility as part of the login process. Privacy information: Google Privacy Policy .

7. Payments and Premium Subscriptions

Website Purchases via Stripe

Website subscriptions are processed through Stripe. Stripe processes payment method data, name, email address, billing address, amount and transaction data in particular. We do not receive complete payment data, but only the information required to process the contract, enable premium access and manage subscriptions, in particular transaction and subscription status.

Provider: Stripe Payments Europe, Limited, 1 Grand Canal Street Lower, Grand Canal Dock, Dublin, D02 H210, Ireland.

The purpose is payment processing, subscription management and fraud prevention. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR; where Stripe processes data for fraud prevention, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR may also apply. Privacy information: Stripe Privacy Policy .

Android Purchases via Google Play

Purchases in the Android app are processed through Google Play Billing. The purchase process takes place with Google. We receive the purchase identifier and the subscription status in order to enable premium access and check the subscription period.

The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Future iOS Purchases

If an iOS version is offered, purchases will be processed through Apple. The provider will then be Apple Distribution International Ltd., Hollyhill Industrial Estate, Hollyhill, Cork, Ireland. This Privacy Policy will be updated before an iOS purchase option becomes available.

10. Storage Periods

  • User account and profile settings: until the account is deleted
  • Game statistics: until the account is deleted
  • Friendship connections, friend codes and online status: until the relevant friendship ends or the account is deleted
  • Leaderboard display and visibility settings: until Community Leaderboard display is disabled, the relevant friendship ends or the account is deleted
  • Lobby and session data: up to 30 days after the end of the lobby
  • Feedback: no longer than 12 months
  • Premium and subscription status: until the end of the contract; thereafter only where legally required
  • Invoice and accounting data: 10 years where tax or commercial-law obligations apply
  • Server logs: generally 30 days

Where statutory retention obligations prevent deletion, processing will be restricted for the duration of the obligation and the data will then be deleted.

11. Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your account in the profile section or send an email with the subject line “Account Deletion” and your registered email address to nextround67@gmail.com. We process requests without delay and generally within one month.

When an account is deleted, the email address, authentication data, player name, profile settings, associated game statistics and associated feedback are deleted in particular, unless legal obligations prevent this.

When your account is deleted, your individual friend code, existing friendship connections, leaderboard visibility settings and the display of your profile in the Community Leaderboard and Friends Leaderboards are also deleted or ended.

An active subscription is not automatically cancelled when the account is deleted. Website subscriptions must be cancelled via the cancellation button on the website or in the designated profile section. Google Play subscriptions must be cancelled through subscription management in the Google Play Store.

Base44 creates automatic backups. Data from accounts that have already been deleted may remain in these backups until the respective backup interval expires. Such data is no longer actively used and is removed during the routine deletion of the backup.
